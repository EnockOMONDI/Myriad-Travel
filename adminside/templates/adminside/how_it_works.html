Myriad admin guide

Where should this package go?

Use this page while adding or editing records. Search a word below, or follow the four steps. The goal is simple: tell Myriad where the trip is, what kind of trip it is, and which pages should show it.

Step 1

Region

The large part of the world. Examples: Africa, Europe, Asia, Middle East, Indian Ocean Islands, Americas.

Use this to place a destination on the correct regional page.

Step 2

Country

The country where the trip takes place. Examples: Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda.

A country is the parent of its cities, parks, beaches, and other destinations.

Step 3

Destination

The specific place a traveller is visiting. Examples: Maasai Mara, Diani, Mombasa, Zanzibar.

Packages should normally use the most specific destination they are about.

Optional

Child destination

Use this when a place sits inside another destination. Example: Lake Nakuru National Park sits inside the wider Nakuru area.

Leave it blank when there is no more specific place.

Special case

Multi-country area

Use this for a travel area that crosses borders, such as Caribbean, Arabian Gulf, or Israel and Jordan.

Turn on multi-country grouping, then link only the existing country records you are sure about.

No matching guide cards. Try “Nakuru”, “collection”, or “deal”.
The package flow

One package can appear in several useful places

Where?Region → Country → Destination
What type?Category, such as safari, honeymoon, or hiking
Which group?Existing collections, such as Kenya Safaris
Is it promoted?Add a campaign if it should appear as a deal

For a multi-country area, use Region → Multi-country area and link its existing countries in the destination form. Its packages can still appear on each linked country page.

Real example

Nakuru and Lake Nakuru

Nakuru is the city or wider area.

Lake Nakuru National Park is the specific travel destination inside Nakuru.

Region: Africa   Collection: Kenya Safaris & Bush

Possible categories: Wildlife Safari, Family Safari, Photography Safari, or Weekend Trip.

A game-drive package should use Lake Nakuru National Park as its main destination. A package about hotels, meetings, or nearby town activities can use Nakuru.

Quick decision

Before you click Save

  • Choose the most specific existing destination that matches the package.
  • Do not create a new country or destination just because the package title mentions a place.
  • Choose every existing collection that should show the package.
  • Use categories to describe the travel style, not the geography.
  • To promote a package, create a campaign and set its dates. The Deals page will show its current status.
