About Myriad Travel

Travel planned with imagination, knowledge and care.

Myriad Travel is a Nairobi-based tour and travel company creating custom experiences across East Africa and beyond.

01 / Our story

Every journey begins with a story.

At Myriad Travel, we believe travel is more than visiting new places. It is about discovering cultures, creating meaningful connections and collecting memories that last a lifetime.

Based in Nairobi, Kenya, we are a passionate team of travel specialists dedicated to creating exceptional travel experiences across Kenya, Africa and the world. From breathtaking wildlife safaris and relaxing beach escapes to international holidays and corporate travel, we bring destinations to life through thoughtful planning, destination expertise and personalized service.

Whether you seek adventure, relaxation, romance or cultural discovery, we take care of the details so you can focus on what matters most — enjoying every moment of your journey.
