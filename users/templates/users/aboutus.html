About the company

What Myriad Travel does.

We plan trips for individuals, families, groups and organizations. Some clients come for a safari, others for a beach break, an outbound holiday, a flight, a visa question or a company retreat. Our work is to make the options clear and handle the moving parts.

Explore Kenya

Chalbi, Amboseli, Maasai Mara, Naivasha, coast, Malindi, Watamu and park-led safari circuits.

Explore The World

Dubai, South Africa, Egypt, Rwanda, Seychelles, Thailand, Schengen Europe and island holidays.

Corporate Movement

Transport, retreats, conferences, team building, executive travel and organization logistics.

Admin Follow-Up
