About Myriad Travel

Travel planned with imagination, knowledge and care.

Myriad Travel is a Nairobi-based tour and travel company creating custom experiences across East Africa and beyond.

From the first conversation to the return journey, we help make the choices clear and the details manageable.

Our story

A travel desk built around real journeys.

Our work brings together destination knowledge, trusted industry relationships and practical follow-through. We design rugged, adventurous, relaxing and romantic trips around each client’s interests and schedule.

Myriad is headquartered in Nairobi and works with a network of hotels, lodges, airlines, transport providers, guides, conservancies and other travel partners.

Our vision

To be a reliable and adventurous travel and tour provider.

Our mission

To deliver remarkable stays and act as a dependable partner to clients and the travel industry.

Where we plan

Kenya, East Africa and carefully planned international journeys.

What guides us

Creativity, honest service, local knowledge and the desire to deliver beyond imagination.
