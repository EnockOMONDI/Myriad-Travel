Visa & Entry Requirements

Prepare the documents and travel authorisations you may need before you depart.

Before you travel, check the entry requirements for your destination and any countries you will transit through. Requirements vary depending on the passport you hold, your travel route, the purpose of your visit and how long you plan to stay.

Confirm whether you need a visa, an electronic travel authorisation or a transit visa. Check your passport validity and whether you need any supporting documents, such as proof of accommodation, a return or onward ticket, or a vaccination certificate. Entry requirements can change, so check with the relevant embassy or immigration authority before booking and again before departure.

Visiting Kenya

Kenya uses an Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) system for visitors. If you are required to obtain an eTA, you must receive approval before you travel. This also applies to children and infants who are required to have one. Use the official Kenya eTA portal to check whether you qualify for an exemption and to apply if needed.

For your application, have a passport valid for at least six months from your planned arrival date in Kenya, with at least one blank page. You will also need a recent photo, your contact details, your arrival and departure itinerary, and your accommodation confirmation. Additional documents may be required depending on your circumstances.
