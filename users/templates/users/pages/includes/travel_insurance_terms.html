Travel with confidence

Whether you’re heading out on a safari in Kenya, taking the family on a beach holiday or travelling abroad for business, travel insurance can help with unexpected costs before or during your trip. Myriad Travel can help you find cover that suits your destination, travel dates and planned activities.

What can travel insurance cover?

  • Emergency medical expenses if you fall ill or are injured during your trip.
  • Emergency medical evacuation if you need to be transferred to a suitable medical facility.
  • Trip cancellation or interruption if you have to cancel or cut your trip short for a reason covered by your policy.
  • Travel delays and baggage issues, including eligible expenses if your journey is delayed or your luggage is lost, stolen or delayed.

Before you buy

Check that your policy covers every traveller, all destinations on your itinerary and your full travel dates. If you’re planning activities such as mountain trekking, scuba diving or a hot-air balloon safari, ask the insurer to confirm they’re covered. For safaris in remote areas, check the emergency medical evacuation benefit. Review the policy limits, exclusions and any rules on pre-existing medical conditions.

Travel insurance is arranged separately unless your Myriad Travel quotation specifically includes it. Cover and claims are subject to the insurer’s policy terms.
