Safari planning guide

Planning your African journey

At Myriad Travel, we believe every journey should be exciting, memorable and stress-free. Whether you're planning a wildlife safari, family holiday, romantic escape or adventure with friends, we've answered some of the most frequently asked questions to help you prepare.

When is the best time to go on safari?+

Kenya offers incredible safari experiences throughout the year. The best time depends on the destinations you wish to visit and the experiences you're looking for.

  • June to October: Generally the dry season and an excellent time for wildlife viewing. The famous Great Wildebeest Migration usually reaches the Maasai Mara during this period, although timing varies.
  • January to March: A wonderful time for wildlife photography, birdwatching and exploring Kenya's national parks.
  • April to May: Traditionally the long-rains season, offering lush landscapes, fewer visitors and sometimes more attractive accommodation rates.
  • November to December: The short-rains period, often offering green scenery, excellent birdwatching and rewarding wildlife encounters.

At Myriad Travel, we help you choose the ideal season based on your interests, travel dates and budget.

Our itineraries can be tailored to combine wildlife, adventure, culture, relaxation and beach holidays.

What should I pack?+

We recommend packing light, comfortable and practical clothing suitable for varying weather conditions.

  • Lightweight clothing in neutral colours
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • A warm jacket for early morning and evening game drives
  • A sunhat, sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Insect repellent

Avoid excessive luggage, particularly if your itinerary includes domestic flights with baggage restrictions.

What are the entry and health requirements?+

International visitors should ensure they have a valid passport and meet the relevant immigration requirements for their destination.

For Kenya, many international travellers require an approved Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) before departure, while certain nationalities and categories of travellers are exempt.

You can check current requirements and apply through Kenya's official eTA portal.

For Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda and other destinations, entry requirements depend on your nationality and itinerary.

Health requirements vary depending on your destination, nationality, travel history and planned activities. A yellow fever vaccination certificate may be required for certain travellers. Malaria prevention may also be advisable for some safari destinations.

We recommend consulting a qualified healthcare professional before travelling.

Private safaris are ideal for families, couples, friends and groups seeking greater flexibility and personalized experiences. We can also help organize special-interest trips, including photography safaris, honeymoons, milestone celebrations and group adventures.

Packing list

Travel Smart. Pack Light. Explore More.

Preparing for your African safari? Packing the right essentials will ensure a comfortable and enjoyable adventure. Whether you're heading out on a wildlife safari, exploring scenic landscapes or embarking on a gorilla trekking experience, here is a simple guide to help you prepare.

01. Clothing & Footwear

  • Lightweight, breathable clothing in neutral colours such as khaki, beige, olive or brown.
  • Long-sleeved shirts and trousers for sun and insect protection.
  • Warm jacket or fleece for chilly mornings and evenings.
  • Light rain jacket for unexpected showers.
  • Comfortable walking shoes or hiking boots.
  • Sandals or casual shoes for relaxing at the lodge.
  • Wide-brimmed hat and sunglasses.
  • Swimwear if your accommodation has a pool.

02. Personal Care & Health

  • Sunscreen and lip balm.
  • Insect repellent.
  • Personal toiletries and hand sanitizer.
  • Prescription medication and a basic first-aid kit.
  • Motion sickness tablets if needed.
  • Any required vaccination documentation.

03. Safari Accessories

  • Camera or smartphone for capturing memories.
  • Binoculars for wildlife viewing.
  • Power bank, chargers and travel adapter.
  • Small backpack for daily excursions.
  • Reusable water bottle.
  • Dust protection for sensitive equipment.

04. Travel Documents & Money

  • Valid passport and necessary travel authorization or visa.
  • Travel insurance details.
  • Flight tickets and booking confirmations.
  • Copies of important travel documents.
  • Credit/debit cards and some local currency for personal expenses and tips.

05. Additional Items for Gorilla Trekking

For travellers visiting Uganda or Rwanda for gorilla trekking, we recommend:

  • Waterproof hiking boots with good grip.
  • Gardening or trekking gloves.
  • Waterproof jacket and backpack cover.
  • Long trousers and gaiters for muddy trails.
  • Walking stick, where needed.

06. Important Packing Tips

  • Pack light: Soft-sided luggage is recommended, particularly for safaris involving small aircraft.
  • Check luggage limits: Domestic and regional flights may have strict baggage restrictions.
  • Dress appropriately: Choose comfortable, practical clothing suitable for outdoor activities and changing weather.
  • Check travel requirements: Confirm entry requirements, vaccinations and insurance before departure.
  • Prepare for remote areas: Carry essentials as some safari destinations have limited shopping facilities and mobile connectivity.

Tipping guide

Understanding tipping on your African safari

At Myriad Travel, we believe that great service makes every journey memorable. Tipping is a thoughtful way to appreciate the guides, drivers and hospitality teams who make your safari experience special.

While tipping is customary in Kenya and many East African destinations, it is entirely voluntary and depends on your satisfaction with the service received.

How much should you tip?
ServiceSuggested tip (USD)
Safari Driver/Guide$10–$20 per guest per day
Lodge & Camp Staff$5–$10 per guest per day
Local or Cultural Guides$5–$15 per guest per activity
Hotel Porters$1–$2 per bag
Housekeeping$2–$5 per room per day
Airport Transfer Drivers$3–$5 per transfer

Tipping during gorilla trekking

  • Trekking Guides: $10–$15 per guest.
  • Porters: $10–$20 per porter, in addition to any porter hire fee.
  • Trackers and Support Teams: Optional contributions, subject to local park guidelines.

Tipping arrangements may vary between destinations, so we recommend checking with your Myriad Travel consultant or local guide.

Useful tipping tips

  • Carry Small Notes: US dollars and local currency are generally accepted. Small denominations are more convenient.
  • Tip at the End: Safari guides and lodge teams are usually tipped at the end of your safari or stay.
  • Use Tip Boxes: Many lodges and camps have shared tip boxes for staff.
  • Check Your Package: Some travel packages may already include gratuities.
  • Tip According to Service: There is no obligation to tip if you are not satisfied with the service.

A little appreciation goes a long way. At Myriad Travel, we are happy to advise you on tipping practices for your specific destination and itinerary.
